FoodieExpress — Privacy & Policy

Clear, user-friendly policy describing how we collect and use data.

Effective: Dec 9, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to FoodieExpress. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it. By using our website or services, you agree to the terms described here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services, personalize your experience, and process orders.

  • Account information: name, email, phone number, delivery address.
  • Order data: items ordered, quantities, timestamps, payment method (we do not store full card data).
  • Device & usage: IP address, browser type, pages visited, cookies.
  • Optional: profile picture, reviews, feedback you submit.

3. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and understand how you use our site.

  • Essential cookies: required for security and sessions.
  • Performance cookies: anonymized analytics to improve the site.
  • Advertising cookies: only if you opt in, used to show relevant offers.

4. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Process and deliver your orders.
  • Communicate order status, offers, and support messages.
  • Improve and personalize the service (recommendations, UX).
  • Prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

5. Sharing & Third Parties

We share data only as necessary:

  • With payment processors to complete transactions.
  • With delivery partners to fulfill orders (address & phone number).
  • When required by law or to protect our rights.

6. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure — please use strong passwords and keep your devices secure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Children

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be communicated via the website or email. Check the Effective date at the top.
